FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

What happens to the data I load?

Your response data is never sent to Aggy's servers. DuckDB Wasm loads, validates, and aggregates it entirely in your browser.

What is a layout JSON?

It defines which question each CSV column belongs to, the question type—such as single-answer, multiple-answer, or numeric—and the labels for response codes. You can download a sample to see the format.

Can I use a CSV from Google Forms or another survey tool?

Yes. Aggy works independently of the collection service as long as you provide a matching layout JSON. CSV column names must match the layout definition.
